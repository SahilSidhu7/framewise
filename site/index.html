How it works

Three steps, no editing vocabulary needed.

You never have to know what a keyframe or a LUT is. Say what you want; the editor does the technical part and shows you a few versions to choose from.

1

Drop in your clips

Phone videos, photos, a song. Long recordings work too: the editor listens to the speech and finds the good parts.

2

Say what you want

"Fun travel recap", "cinematic", "30-second TikTok". Pick a style and where it's going, or leave it on Auto.

3

Pick a version

Preview the options, keep the one you like, tweak anything by hand. Every AI change is one undo away.

What it does today

Built for people who post, and people who automate.

Long video to reels

One interview or stream in, several vertical clips out: best moments first, pauses and filler words cut, the speaker kept in frame, captions on.

Captions that stay out of the way

Word-by-word highlighted captions that move off faces and away from titles already in the video. Exports an .srt as well.

Edit by prompt

Cuts on the beat, transitions, colour looks, titles and music from one sentence, with style and output options next to the prompt.

Photo fixes

Exposure, white balance and colour casts corrected, with looks for food, portraits, products and more.

Layered timeline

Overlays, picture-in-picture, blend modes, opacity and keyframes underneath, for when you want full control.

Automation

Your PC becomes a clipping engine.

Run AIEditor without the window and drive it from n8n, Zapier, scripts or AI agents. Cloud clipping tools charge per minute of video; this runs on hardware you already own.

n8nCommunity node and ready-made workflow: video in, captioned shorts out.
HTTPJob API with API keys, uploads, long-polling and signed webhooks.
CLIOne command per task, for scripts and scheduled jobs.
FoldersDrop a file into a watched folder, get the edit back next to it.
MCPAI agents can call the same recipes on local files.
# one long video in, three TikTok reels out
aieditor reel talk.mp4 --platform tiktok --set count=3 --out shorts/

# captions burned in, plus an .srt
aieditor captions interview.mp4 --set style=boxed

# headless API for n8n on http://127.0.0.1:8765
aieditor serve

# from any tool that speaks HTTP
curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/v1/jobs \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -d '{"recipe":"reel","inputs":["f_..."],
       "style":"energetic_reel",
       "output":{"platform":"tiktok","length":30},
       "webhook":"https://n8n.example/hook"}'

Questions

Good to know

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC. A recent CPU works; an NVIDIA graphics card makes exports faster. A typical 1080p reel takes about a minute on a laptop CPU.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Editing, speech recognition and the AI all run locally. The only network traffic is what you set up yourself, such as a webhook to your own n8n.

How does the Pro licence work offline?

A Pro key is digitally signed. The app checks the signature on your machine, so it keeps working without internet and there is no licence server to go down.

Can I use it with n8n in the cloud?

Yes, by giving cloud n8n a secure way to reach the engine on your PC (for example a tunnel with its own login). The editor itself never needs a server of ours.

Is this good enough for professional work?

The automatic edits are designed to look finished out of the box. For frame-accurate work, the layered timeline and every AI change are fully editable by hand.
