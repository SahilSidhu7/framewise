What it does

The open CapCut, Photoshop and After Effects alternative.

01

Clip

One long video in, short clips out. Best moments ranked by speech, sound and picture; pauses and filler words cut; the speaker kept in frame; word-timed captions.

02

Edit photos

"Warm golden hour, make the colours pop." The local AI picks the looks and settings, and you can change every one of them by hand.

03

Composite Next

Layers, blend modes, masks and track mattes on a GPU compositor, for photo retouching and motion graphics in the same tool.

Features

The tedious parts, done for you.

  • Reels from long videoBest moments first, pauses and filler words removed, the speaker kept in frame for 9:16.
  • CaptionsWord-timed, with the spoken word highlighted. Placed clear of faces and on-screen titles; .srt export included.
  • ColourExposure and white-balance fixes plus looks for travel, food, portraits and film.
  • TimelineLayers, overlays, blend modes, opacity and keyframes when you want to take over.
  • ExportPlatform presets for TikTok, Reels, Shorts and YouTube. NVIDIA hardware encoding when available.

Automation

Run it from n8n, scripts or agents.

Start the engine without a window and send it jobs. Cloud clipping services bill per minute of video; this runs on hardware you already own, free and without limits.

n8n nodeREST APISigned webhooksCLIWatch foldersMCP
# one long video in, three TikTok reels out
framewise reel talk.mp4 --platform tiktok --set count=3 --out shorts/

# captions burned in, plus an .srt
framewise captions interview.mp4 --set style=boxed

# headless API on 127.0.0.1:8765
framewise serve

curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/v1/jobs \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" \
  -d '{"recipe":"reel","inputs":["f_..."],
       "output":{"platform":"tiktok","length":30},
       "webhook":"https://n8n.example/hook"}'

FAQ

Before you install

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 with 8 GB of memory or more. Any recent processor works; an NVIDIA graphics card speeds up exports. On an 8-core laptop a 30-second 1080p reel exports in about three minutes, and the app measures your PC during setup.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Editing, transcription and the editing model all run locally. The only network traffic is what you set up yourself, such as a webhook to your own n8n.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, trial or feature limit, and exports have no watermark. The project is funded by sponsors and by optional extra packs, never by locking the editor.

What licence is it under?

The GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. You can use it for any purpose, including commercial work. If you distribute a modified version, or run one as a service for others, you share its source under the same licence.

Can cloud n8n reach it?

Yes, through a secure tunnel to your PC that has its own authentication. The editor itself never needs a server of ours.

Can I edit by hand?

Always. AI photo edits and generated clips are ordinary edits and timelines that you can change one by one.
